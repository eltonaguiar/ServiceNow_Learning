How to use this in an afternoon

1. Read the ELI5 guide (20 min). 2. Get a free PDI at developer.servicenow.com and run Lab 00 (30 min). 3. Pick one module, render its brief with python3 scripts/sn_learn.py brief itsm-incident, and run the loop. 4. For SAM specifically, start at the SAM setup guide.

What is verified, and what is not

Every guide cites official ServiceNow sources and links out rather than copying them. Menu paths, field lists and "does guided setup exist" are marked verify wherever they vary by release and entitlement — the probe resolves them against your instance. Timeboxes are targets, not measured benchmarks. Nothing here has been executed against a licensed SAM instance: the SAM material is documentation-grounded, and the lab says so.
