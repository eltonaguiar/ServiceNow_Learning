CONCEPT: ServiceNow Incident Management

1. WHAT IS IT?
   ServiceNow Incident Management is the process of logging, tracking, and resolving unplanned interruptions to IT services.

2. WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?
   It ensures that when something breaks, it gets logged, assigned, and fixed quickly so business operations aren't disrupted.

3. HOW DO I USE IT?
   Step 1: Navigate to Incident → All to see existing incidents
   Step 2: Click New to create a new incident with short_description and priority
   Step 3: Assign the incident to an assignment group and resolve it when fixed

4. WHERE DOES IT LIVE?
   Incident → All (in the Application Navigator)

5. WHAT TABLE DOES IT USE?
   incident

6. WHAT ROLE DO I NEED?
   itil

7. WHAT BROKE AND HOW DID I FIX IT?
   I couldn't assign an incident because I didn't have the itil role. I added the role to my user, logged out and back in, and it worked.
