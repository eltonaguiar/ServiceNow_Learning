### Fields — critical vs useful vs optional

**Critical** (get these wrong and the module misbehaves; mandatory-ness varies by instance):

- `territory` — Primary routing constraint; wrong territory = unassignable work.
- `scheduled start/end` — Drives the whole scheduling engine and customer promise.
- `assigned_agent / crew` — Who actually does the work.
- `task type + skills` — Skill matching decides eligibility.
- `location (geo)` — Travel time and dispatch map depend on coordinates.

**Useful:** `priority`, estimated duration, parts required, customer signature

**Optional / nice to have:** billing codes, equipment details

**Related lists worth configuring:** `Tasks`, `Assignments`, `Parts`, Time entries

## Must know (the 5 facts that unlock the rest)

- Work order = the business ask; task = the visit; assignment = who/when. Three tables, three concepts.
- Constraints (skills, territory, hours) are configuration, not code — but bad data makes the engine look broken.
- Mobile behaviour must be tested on Now Mobile, not desktop.

## Common mistakes

- No territories (then everything is unschedulable)
- Skipping skill data
- Testing only as dispatcher
