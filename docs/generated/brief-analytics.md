### Fields — critical vs useful vs optional

**Critical** (get these wrong and the module misbehaves; mandatory-ness varies by instance):

- `indicator source (table + aggregate)` — Count/avg of what, filtered how. Vague source = untrustworthy number.
- `conditions (filter)` — Most 'wrong numbers' are wrong filters, not wrong math.
- `breakdown` — The 'by' dimension; without it a number cannot drive action.
- `frequency + collector schedule` — Daily vs weekly decides whether you see trends or snapshots.
- `target / threshold` — Turns a number into an alert.

**Useful:** `forecasting`, unit/format (%, hours), visibility domains (who can see what)

**Optional / nice to have:** scorecard widgets, custom widgets

**Related lists worth configuring:** Indicator breakdowns, `Targets`, Dashboards containing this indicator

## Must know (the 5 facts that unlock the rest)

- Indicator = what to measure. Breakdown = how to slice it. Dashboard = how to show it. That is the entire model.
- PA needs a data collector to keep scores current; nothing is real-time by default.
- Analytics visibility (who sees which rows) is separate from ACLs and is a common leak.
- Platform Analytics (newer) and Performance Analytics (classic) coexist; don't assume docs match your release.

## Common mistakes

- Building 40 indicators instead of 3 that someone will act on.
- Reporting on data that is dirty upstream (report the truth, then fix the data).
- Forgetting the collector schedule, then wondering why numbers froze.
- Publishing dashboards to users who cannot see the underlying rows.
