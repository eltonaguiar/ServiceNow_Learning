### Fields — critical vs useful vs optional

**Critical** (get these wrong and the module misbehaves; mandatory-ness varies by instance):

- `subject_person` — The employee the case is about — distinct from the opener. Wrong value leaks or hides data.
- `hr_service` — Selects the workflow, SLA, and fields. The single most important choice on the form.
- `state` — HRSD states include work-in-progress/awaiting-info; alert/notification logic keys off them.
- `opened_for / opened_by` — Confidentiality depends on the who.
- `assignment_group (HR COE)` — Routing between HR COEs; no group = no service.

**Useful:** `priority`, due date, confidential flag, `short_description`

**Optional / nice to have:** additional comments, `attachments`, requested_for department fields

**Related lists worth configuring:** `Tasks`, `Documents`, `Approvals`, Related cases

## Must know (the 5 facts that unlock the rest)

- Confidentiality in HRSD is ACLs + restricted fields, not a checkbox. Design it first.
- HR Services (not 'categories') are the catalog of what employees can request; each carries its own workflow.
- Lifecycle Events orchestrate many cases/tasks across departments (onboarding day 1 ≠ one case).

## Common mistakes

- Reusing ITIL assignment groups for HR COEs.
- Ignoring confidentiality until after go-live (retrofitting ACLs is a rebuild).
- Building one giant 'ask HR' service instead of a catalog.
