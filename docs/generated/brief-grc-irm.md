### Fields — critical vs useful vs optional

**Critical** (get these wrong and the module misbehaves; mandatory-ness varies by instance):

- `risk statement + entity scope` — A risk without an entity is not auditable.
- `inherent vs residual score` — The whole methodology lives in that pair.
- `control owner` — Accountability; attestations route to this person.
- `test result + evidence` — Compliance claim without evidence fails audit.
- `due dates` — Drive escalations and regulatory reporting.

**Useful:** risk appetite thresholds, control objective, policy → control mapping, issue linkage

**Optional / nice to have:** custom scoring matrices, advanced risk hierarchy

**Related lists worth configuring:** `Controls`, `Assessments`, `Issues`, `Evidence`, `Entities`

## Must know (the 5 facts that unlock the rest)

- Build order: entities → risks → controls → tests → issues. Any other order creates orphan data.
- IRM tables are the least 'guessable' on the platform; use the guided setup as the curriculum, not the docs.
- Scoring matrices are configuration; changing them after assessments exist rewrites your history.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with controls instead of entities and risks.
- Treating a GRC implementation as a data-entry project (it is a methodology decision).
- No evidence attachments — fails audit regardless of perfect status fields.
